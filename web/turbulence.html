Turbulence index · rolling 24 hours

Conditions

Index—reading
W · passengers negative—share of wallets that traded and ended down
D · flights lost—share of launches departed or cancelled

Method

Over the last 24 hours, W is the share of wallets that traded on a Pons curve and ended the window with a negative realized result, and D is the share of tokens launched in the window that are departed or cancelled. The index is round(100 × (0.6 W + 0.4 D)), so passengers weigh more than flights. Labels: 0–20 Clear skies, 20–40 Light chop, 40–60 Moderate, 60–80 Severe, 80–100 Extreme.
