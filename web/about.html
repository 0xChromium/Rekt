About

67% of Robinhood Chain traders lost money. Find out how much you contributed.

rekt.report is the loss ledger of Robinhood Chain. Paste any address and get a boarding pass: how much it lost on Pons, on what, to whom, and where it ranks. Now boarding: everyone. Destination: zero. On time.

How it works

  • Boarding. Every token launched on the Pons v2 factory is a flight. We read the launch, the graduation and every buy and sell on its bonding curve, chain-wide, as it happens, from the public RPC.
  • The flight. A wallet's buys and sells fold into one position per token. Realized result = what you sold for, less what you paid for the part you sold: quote_out − quote_in × min(1, tokens_out / tokens_in). Bags still held are valued separately and marked approximate.
  • The pass. Net realized result across every position, converted to dollars with the daily price book; rank among wallets with three or more trades; class by percentile; worst flight and the airline that flew it; badges; and how many other passengers never arrived on the same flight.
  • The board. A token is boarding from launch, arrived when it graduates to the pool, departed after ten minutes without a trade at a price at or below a tenth of its peak, and cancelled when that happens inside the first ten minutes.
  • Turbulence. Over a rolling 24 hours, W is the share of wallets that traded and ended negative, D the share of launches that departed or were cancelled; the index is round(100 × (0.6 W + 0.4 D)). Method and today's reading.

Disclaimers

  • Dollar figures are approximate. Every quote asset is converted with a price book refreshed about hourly; positions in an asset we cannot price are left out and counted separately.
  • Curve trades only, for now. Trades on the Uniswap v4 pool after graduation are not folded yet; they are on the roadmap.
  • Records begin at the first folded block, stated on every pass. Older history extends backwards as the fold catches up.
  • None of this is financial advice, a recommendation, or an invitation to trade anything.
  • No payouts are tied to losses. There are no refunds and no prizes. The compensation desk pays a share of $REKT's creator fees to holders who stake, and nothing else. The rule, verbatim.
  • Addresses are wallets, not people. We name no one. Addresses are shown truncated and link to Blockscout.

The token

$REKT is launched on Pons v2 with a 1.5% creator tax. The contract address appears in the amber strip at the top of every page the moment it exists; until then, anything claiming to be $REKT is not ours.

Code

Open source, MIT. Node 22, SQLite, one process for the watcher and one for the site. The board is a split-flap; the sound is one synthesised click and stays off until you press the speaker.
