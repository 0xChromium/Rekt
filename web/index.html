How it works

1 · Boarding

Every token launched on Pons v2 is a flight. We read every buy and sell on its curve, chain-wide, as it happens.

2 · The flight

Each wallet's trades fold into a position per token. What you sold for, less what you paid for what you sold, is your realized result, in dollars at the price of the day.

3 · The pass

Your pass sums it up: net result, rank among passengers with three or more trades, worst flight and who cancelled it. Destination: zero. On time.
