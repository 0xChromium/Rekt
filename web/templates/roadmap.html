What the coin buys

$REKT buys a share of the fees through the compensation desk, and nothing else. Half of every creator fee the token earns goes to holders who stake, on-chain, from the block the splitter contract becomes the fee recipient; what the token earns before that block pays for building it. There are no tiers, no paid features and no early access; the site stays free for every passenger, holder or not.
