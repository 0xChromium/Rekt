The rule

Compensation desk open.

Half of every creator fee $REKT earns goes to holders who stake, starting the moment the splitter contract becomes the fee recipient. What the token earns before that transaction pays for building it and is the team's. The switch is one transaction on the Pons factory, so anyone can see the exact block the rule starts at. After it, only the factory owner could move the recipient, with a three-day timelock.
